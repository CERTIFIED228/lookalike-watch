lookalike-watch

Warns you when a site is pretending to be another one, or is on a list of known phishing sites. Everything is checked on this computer; the sites you visit are never sent anywhere.

How severe is a warning?

CRITICALOn a confirmed phishing list, or looks identical to a brand or one of your sites (e.g. a Cyrillic "а" in paypal).
HIGHA one-letter typo of a protected brand, the brand plus a scam word (paypal-login, hmrc-tax-refund), or a real domain used as a fake subdomain.
MEDIUMWeaker signs, such as a popular site's name on free hosting. Only shown on pages asking for a password or card.
LOWGeneral warning signs (raw IP address, unencrypted login, mixed alphabets). Only shown on login pages.
